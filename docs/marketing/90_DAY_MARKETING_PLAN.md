# ConstructFlow — 90-Day Go-To-Market Plan

**Owner:** Founder-led GTM
**Objective:** 10 paying companies, ≤10% 30-day churn, and validated positioning + pricing by Day 90.

---

## Positioning (the spine of this plan)

> ConstructFlow turns a contractor's Excel BOQ into a live profit system.
> **Know your cost. Bill faster. Protect your margin.**

We do not market "construction project management software." We market the answer to four money questions:

1. Where is my money?
2. Which project is losing money?
3. What can I bill right now?
4. What has not been paid yet?

Every activity below — outreach, demo, content, pricing — repeats this one story. The killer feature we lead with is the **BOQ → Budget → Actual Cost → Change Order → Billing → Profit** loop.

## The Funnel (canonical numbers)

> 500 prospects → 200 qualified → 100 conversations → 40 demos → 20 pilots → **10 paying**

These numbers replace the previous per-phase targets and are the single source of truth.

| Stage | Definition | Target rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Prospect → Qualified | Matches ICP, 3+ active projects, buying signal | 40% |
| Qualified → Conversation | Discovery call booked & held | 50% |
| Conversation → Demo | Demo delivered | 40% |
| Demo → Pilot | 30-day pilot accepted | 50% |
| Pilot → Paid | Converts to founding-customer plan | 50% |

### Guardrails (fail = pivot, don't grind)
- **Day 30:** fewer than 100 conversations → channel/offer problem, fix before scaling volume.
- **Day 60:** demo→pilot below 30% → demo or messaging problem; pilot→paid below 30% → product or pricing problem.

## Channels (named, not aspirational)

Philippine construction SMBs live on:

1. **Facebook construction groups** — Philippine contractor, foreman, and QS communities.
2. **Messenger / Viber** — the default business chat in PH; primary direct-outreach channel.
3. **LinkedIn** — owners, project managers, quantity surveyors.
4. **Referrals from suppliers** — hardware depots and material suppliers see everyone's pain.
5. **Site visits** — walk onto active job sites with a printed one-page cost-tracking offer.
6. **Content** — one educational post per week on profitability, not features.

**Budget:** founder time is the primary spend. Paid ads are tested **only after** organic conversion is demonstrated (see Day 76–90).

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Days 1–14)

Lock the fundamentals before any outreach volume.

- Finalize the [ICP](IDEAL_CUSTOMER_PROFILE.md) and pick the lead niche (start with general contractors).
- Interview 10 contractors — document workflows, find the top three painful problems.
- Write the positioning paragraph above onto the landing page verbatim.
- Build the prospect tracker (see [Sales Pipeline](SALES_PIPELINE.md) fields).
- Build the first **500-company** prospect list (not 100 — the funnel requires it).
- Create a realistic demo account seeded with a real construction scenario.
- Prepare the 15-minute demo using [Demo Script](DEMO_SCRIPT.md).

**Exit gate:** ICP documented, 500 prospects in tracker, demo rehearsed end-to-end.

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Outreach (Days 15–30)

Run the funnel, but *interview before pitching*.

**Targets:** 200 qualified · 100 conversations · 40 demos · 10 pilots started.

- Run outreach from [Outreach Templates](OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md) across named channels above.
- Ask workflow questions before pitching: what does the problem cost? what would they pay?
- Record objections and current software in the tracker.
- Book and hold discovery calls using [Discovery Call Script](DISCOVERY_CALL_SCRIPT.md).

**Cadence:** 15–20 qualified conversations per week, not 20 cold messages per day.

---

## Phase 2 — MVP Validation (Days 31–45)

- Prioritize features strictly from interview evidence.
- Fix onboarding friction before adding pilots.
- Invite 10–20 companies into a 30-day pilot.
- Track activation by account.

**Activation events (a company is "active" when):**
1. Company created
2. First client created
3. First project created
4. First BOQ/budget imported
5. First daily report submitted
6. First expense recorded
7. First photo uploaded

**Exit gate:** ≥10 pilots live and ≥50% of pilot accounts hit 4+ activation events.

---

## Phase 3 — Beta (Days 46–60): The Decision Gate

- Run weekly customer calls; measure usage and churn risk.
- Collect testimonials and identify must-have features.
- Remove anything nobody used in the pilot.

**Goal:** ≥20 pilots total, ≥10 active.

**Decision gate (Day 60) — PIVOT or SCALE:**
- Demo→pilot ≥ 30% **and** pilot→paid ≥ 30% → proceed to conversion.
- Below either → fix demo/messaging or product/pricing before spending more on volume.

---

## Phase 4 — Paid Conversion (Days 61–75)

- Present the founding-customer offer and [pricing](PRICING_AND_OFFER.md) to active pilots.
- Run conversion calls; ask directly for payment.
- Record objections — price vs. value vs. missing feature.
- Improve onboarding to shorten time-to-first-BOQ.

**Goal:** first 5 paying companies (the funnel's 50% pilot→paid).

---

## Phase 5 — Scale (Days 76–90)

Double down **only** on the channel that produced the most paying customers.

- Increase volume on the winning channel; cut the rest.
- Launch a referral program for the first paying customers.
- Publish 2–3 customer stories; refresh the landing page with real quotes.
- Continue weekly demos.

**Goal:** 10 paying companies.

---

## Retention (invisible in the old plan — now a goal)

- **Target:** ≤10% 30-day churn.
- Weekly check: are paying customers still logging in and billing?
- First sign of disengagement triggers a founder call within 48 hours.

## Day 60 Pivot/Scale Scorecard

Score each row honestly. **Green = scale, amber = fix then re-test, red = pivot.**

| Signal | Scale (green) | Fix then re-test (amber) | Pivot (red) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Demos delivered | ≥ 40 | 20–39 | < 20 |
| Demo → Pilot | ≥ 40% | 30–39% | < 30% |
| Pilot → Paid | ≥ 40% | 30–39% | < 30% |
| Pilot activation (4+ events) | ≥ 60% | 40–59% | < 40% |
| Weekly active pilot companies | ≥ 10 | 5–9 | < 5 |
| Objection clarity | One dominant objection identified | Mixed objections | No pattern |
| Winning channel | 1 channel = 50%+ of demos | 2 channels close | No clear source |

**Decision rules:**
- 5+ green and 0 red → **SCALE.**
- Any red on *Demo → Pilot* or *Pilot → Paid* → fix product/pricing/messaging before more volume.
- 3+ red → **PIVOT** — reposition or re-niche before spending further.

**Pivot options (ranked):**
1. Re-niche to a sharper segment (e.g., fit-out contractors only).
2. Re-message around the single strongest money question.
3. Re-price or restructure the [founding offer](PRICING_AND_OFFER.md).
4. Change the wedge feature (lead with Billing, not BOQ).

This scorecard is the formal Day 60 decision record — fill it in and date it. It feeds the weekly review in [Marketing Metrics](MARKETING_METRICS.md).
