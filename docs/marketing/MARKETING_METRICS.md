# ConstructFlow Marketing Metrics

## Acquisition
- Prospects added
- Outreach sent
- Response rate
- Conversations
- Demos
- Demo conversion rate

## Activation
- New accounts
- Projects created
- Daily reports created
- Expenses created
- Photos uploaded
- Weekly active companies

## Revenue
- Trials
- Paid conversions
- MRR
- Average revenue per account
- Churn
- Expansion revenue

## Funnel Target
500 prospects → 100 conversations → 40 demos → 20 pilots → 10 paying customers

## Weekly Review
Every Sunday record:
- What worked?
- What did not work?
- Which channel generated the best leads?
- What objections appeared most often?
- What feature was requested most?
- What caused customers to convert?
- What caused customers to leave?

## North Star Metric
For the first 90 days:

**Number of paying construction companies actively using ConstructFlow.**

Do not optimize for social followers or website traffic before product-market fit.
