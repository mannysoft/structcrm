# Pricing and Founding Customer Offer

## Initial Pricing Hypothesis

### Starter — $49/month
- Up to 3 active projects
- Up to 5 users
- Core project management
- Daily reports
- Basic expenses

### Professional — $99/month
- Up to 15 active projects
- Up to 20 users
- Advanced reports
- Client portal
- Additional project controls

### Business — $199/month
- Unlimited projects
- Unlimited users
- Advanced reporting
- Priority support
- Advanced features

Pricing is a hypothesis and should be validated.

## Founding Customer Program
Offer the first 10 companies:
- 30-day pilot
- Personal onboarding
- Direct founder support
- Founding-customer pricing
- Product feedback channel

Avoid promising lifetime free access.

## Pricing Questions
- What do you currently pay for software?
- What does your current process cost in staff time?
- What would solving this problem be worth monthly?
- Which features would justify a higher plan?

## Handling Price Objections
Do not discount automatically.

Ask:
"What were you expecting to pay?"

Determine whether the issue is price, value, missing functionality, or lack of urgency.
