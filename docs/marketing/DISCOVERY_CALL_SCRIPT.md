# Customer Discovery Call Script

## Goal
Understand the customer's workflow and pain before demonstrating ConstructFlow.

## Opening
Thanks for taking the time. I'm researching how small and medium construction companies manage projects. I'm not going to spend this call trying to sell you software. I'd mainly like to understand how your current process works.

## Questions
### Company
- How many people are in the company?
- How many projects do you normally have active?

### Project Management
- How do you create and track projects?
- Who is responsible for project updates?
- How do you know whether a project is on schedule?

### Field Reporting
- How does the site supervisor report what happened each day?
- Where are those reports stored?
- How do you share photos?

### Costs
- How do you track project expenses?
- How do you know whether a project is profitable?
- How quickly can you identify overspending?

### Communication
- What tools do employees use?
- How much project information is stored in chat messages?

### Existing Software
- What software are you using?
- What do you like?
- What do you dislike?
- Why haven't you changed?

### Pain
- What is the most frustrating part of managing several projects?
- What administrative task takes the most time?
- What happens when project information is missed?

### Buying
- Have you paid for construction software before?
- Who makes the purchasing decision?
- What would make a new system worth paying for?

## Closing
Based on what you've told me, I see a few areas where we may be able to help.

Would you be interested in seeing an early version?

## Rule
Do not argue with the customer or tell them what problem they have. Let them describe it.
