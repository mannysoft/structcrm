# ConstructFlow Marketing Strategy

## Objective
Acquire the first 10 paying construction companies within 90 days while validating the strongest customer segment, problem, positioning, and pricing.

## Target Customer
Small and medium construction companies with approximately 5–50 employees and multiple active projects.

Initial focus:
- General contractors
- Residential builders
- Renovation contractors
- Commercial fit-out contractors
- Specialty contractors managing several concurrent jobs

## Positioning
ConstructFlow helps small and medium construction companies manage projects, daily reports, tasks, photos, expenses, and client updates in one place.

## Primary Message
Stop managing construction projects through spreadsheets and scattered chat messages.

## 90-Day Goals
- 500+ prospects identified
- 200 qualified prospects
- 100 direct conversations
- 40 product demos
- 20 trials/pilots
- 10 paying customers

## Channels
1. Direct outreach
2. Facebook construction communities
3. LinkedIn
4. Referrals
5. Construction directories
6. Content marketing

Paid advertising should be tested only after organic conversion is demonstrated.

## Offer
Early customers receive guided onboarding, a 30-day pilot, direct founder support, founding-customer pricing, and an opportunity to influence product development.

## Funnel
Prospect → Conversation → Discovery → Demo → Pilot → Paid → Referral

## Key Metrics
Track weekly:
- Prospects
- Responses
- Conversations
- Demos
- Trials
- Active accounts
- Paying customers
- MRR
- Churn
- Top requested feature
- Main objection

## Principle
Optimize for contractors who actively use the product and pay for it, not followers.
