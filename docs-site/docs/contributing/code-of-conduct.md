# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want StructCRM to be a project where anyone can contribute regardless of experience
level, background, identity or employer. That means a working environment that is
professional, patient and focused on the work.

## Expected behaviour

- **Assume good intent.** Ask before concluding someone was careless.
- **Critique the change, not the person.** "This query scopes by company twice" is useful;
  "you clearly don't understand multi-tenancy" is not.
- **Be specific.** Reproductions, versions and logs help more than opinions.
- **Accept that maintainers say no.** Scope, timing and architectural fit are legitimate
  reasons to decline a contribution.
- **Respect confidentiality.** Never paste another company's data, credentials or private
  logs into an issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument.
- Dismissing reports of problems in the above categories.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages, documentation and any
project space where you represent StructCRM.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers — use the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** channel if you need a private route, or contact a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Include what happened, where, when, and who was involved. Screenshots help.

## Enforcement

| Severity | Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| Minor, first occurrence | Private note explaining the problem and the expected behaviour |
| Repeated or moderate | Warning, with a temporary limit on participation |
| Serious or sustained | Temporary or permanent ban from project spaces |

Maintainers recuse themselves from handling reports in which they are involved. Decisions are
made in the interest of the community, not of any individual.

## Attribution

enforcement structure.
